<details>
<summary><strong>Details</strong></summary>

<ul>
<li>Collected oven opening trajectories from a multi-contact planner for a quadruped robot with an arm.</li>
<li>Trained an auto-regressive high-level generative model (VQ-BeT) to imitate the collected trajectories.</li>
<li>Trained a low-level reinforcement learning model to track the high-level command.</li>
<li>Achieved a visual-input multi-model oven opening and closing framework in simulation.</li>
</ul>

</details>
